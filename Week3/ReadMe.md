# AnalystLab Africa — Week 3 Advanced Data Analysis

## Project Overview
This project is a continuation of my Week 2 Superstore Sales Business Intelligence project during the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship.

The Week 3 objective was to perform deeper analysis, develop meaningful KPIs and DAX measures, and build a more comprehensive interactive Power BI report for decision-making.

## Business Questions
- How are sales and profit performing?
- How is performance changing over time?
- Which customers and segments contribute to business performance?
- How do regions and geographic areas compare?
- Which products require deeper profitability investigation?
- How should discount information be interpreted alongside profit and margin?

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Performance
Executive KPIs, slicers, monthly sales trend and high-level sales/profit analysis.

### 2. Customer & Performance Analysis
Customer, segment, regional, quarterly and product performance analysis.

### 3. Growth & Profitability Analysis
Yearly sales/profit/growth matrix, geographic analysis and category/sub-category profitability and discount analysis.
